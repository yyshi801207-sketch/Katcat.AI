# Code of conduct / 社区行为准则

Be respectful, discuss the work rather than attacking people, and welcome contributors with different experience levels. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and sharing another person's private information are unacceptable. Keep discussions relevant and avoid spam.

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, close disruptive discussions, and restrict participation for serious or repeated violations. These standards also apply to maintainers. Report incidents through a private channel listed on the [owner's profile](https://github.com/yyshi801207-sketch), if available; otherwise request private contact through an issue without personal details. No dedicated confidential reporting service is promised.

请尊重他人，讨论问题本身，欢迎不同经验水平的贡献者。禁止骚扰、歧视、威胁、人身攻击、泄露隐私和垃圾信息。维护者可移除不当内容、关闭破坏性讨论，对严重或反复违规者限制参与；这些规则同样适用于维护者。报告事件可通过[仓库所有者主页](https://github.com/yyshi801207-sketch)公布的私密渠道联系；若没有，请发不含个人信息的 Issue 请求私下联系。本项目未承诺专门的保密举报服务。
